Our view Congress cannot be sued for being Congress. A dirty voter list into court in 90 days. Step by step.

Congress cannot be sued for being Congress. The Speech or Debate Clause in Article I, Section 6 and the standing rule in Lujan v. Defenders of Wildlife close that door. A dirty voter registration list into federal court. 52 U.S.C. § 20510 gives an aggrieved person a private right of action under the National Voter Registration Act. Written notice goes to the chief election official of the State. If the violation is not corrected in ninety days, the case may be filed in district court for an injunction. The court may award fees if the plaintiff prevails.

Our view The tent does not need a palace. It needs a clean roll and a gavel. Clean the roll. Keep the gavel.

List maintenance is 52 U.S.C. § 20507. A noncitizen who votes in a federal election commits a crime under 18 U.S.C. § 611. Unlawful voting is a deportable offense under 8 U.S.C. § 1227(a)(6). Names go to the United States Attorney. The United States Attorney files. That is the lawful path. It is slow. It is real.

Bringing in and harboring certain aliens is 8 U.S.C. § 1324. Records of federal grants sit under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552. A class action that claims to vacate the House will be dismissed. Powell v. McCormack and Luther v. Borden already said why. Anyone selling that suit is selling a dismissal.
