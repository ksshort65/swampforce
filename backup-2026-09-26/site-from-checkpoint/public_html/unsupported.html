Unsupported claims

4 claims were reviewed and could not be tied to a primary source. They are kept apart from the 118 verified cases on Fake News Exposed.

Unsupported

A state secretary of state said dead people and noncitizens should vote.

Pushed by A television statement (about September 2026); the broadcast was not identified

Why it is unsupported No record of the secretary saying this was found. Her documented statements describe removing deceased voters using state health and Social Security death data, and rejecting noncitizen registrations. Her office's 2022 mailing to about 30,000 noncitizens was a mailing error: registration-information postcards, not ballots or forms.

Unsupported

"EXPOSED: Democrats' Big Oil Payoff Scheme... FBI Subpoenas Incoming" (gas prices are "not the Iran war")

Pushed by X post by @TrumpNews, byline "Jonathan Gregory", Sep 23, 2026

Why it is unsupported No DOJ or FBI press release, court filing or other record of such subpoenas was found. The same byline appeared on an earlier unsupported "seven FBI agents vs. Pelosi" story. The post's price figures roughly match EIA, but its claim that high prices are "not the Iran war" contradicts EIA and IEA data showing a war-driven shortage of refined products. FEC records show oil-industry PAC money going mostly to Republicans. Details: Gas Price Gap Tracker (gas-gap.html#gg-6).

UnsupportedFormerly case 102

Trump ordered the cutting down of iconic weeping willow trees outside the Kennedy Center.

Pushed by Adam Parkhomenko (former Clinton adviser); viral social posts

Why it is unsupported Searched: sworn hearing transcript (Beatty v. Trump, D.D.C. 1:25-cv-04480, Doc 45-1, pp. 26-28), the DC Preservation League v. Kennedy Center opinion (1:26-cv-00981, Doc 45, May 29, 2026) and plaintiffs' brief (Doc 40). On-record denial: Kennedy Center executive Matthew Floca, under oath: "The trees were taken out because they were falling down ... we took them out because of a maintenance need"; asked about the chairman's tree preference: "[President Trump and I] haven't specifically talked about the weeping willows or the replacement" (quoted in Doc 45 at Hr'g Tr. 28:17-19). No record, filing, order or statement produced shows Trump ordered the removal. Context: the court remarked that the planned changes "seem based purely on the personal preferences of President Trump and Mr. Floca" (about the replacement species and columns).

UnsupportedFormerly case 246

Politico/NYT/WaPo/CNN/AP/Reuters/WSJ reported Trump fired Comey shortly after Comey requested more Russia-probe resources; DOJ/Rosenstein/McCabe said that was untrue and probe was adequately resourced.

Pushed by Politico; New York Times; Washington Post; CNN; AP; Reuters; Wall Street Journal

Why it is unsupported Claim (anonymous-sourced, May 10, 2017): Comey requested more Russia-probe resources days before he was fired. On-record denial under oath: Acting FBI Director Andrew McCabe, SSCI Worldwide Threats hearing, May 11, 2017 (S. Hrg. 115-205): "I cannot confirm that request was made ... I'm not aware of that request and it's not consistent with my understanding of how we request additional resources ... I strongly believe that the Russia investigation is adequately resourced." Searched: the S. Hrg. 115-205 transcript and Comey's June 8, 2017 SSCI testimony (CHRG-115shrg25890: 0 mentions of "resources"). No document, testimony or named source substantiating the request appears in those records.
