Article V: the states’ way to fix Congress

How it works

34

state legislatures (two-thirds of 50) apply. Congress “shall call” a convention.

38

states (three-fourths) must ratify any amendment it proposes, by legislature or state convention.

Convention of States: where it stands

20 of 34 states have passed the Convention of States resolution, by the advocacy group’s own count Convention of States Action (advocacy group) list ↗. We checked 20 of 20 against an official record: the application as received by the U.S. House Clerk, or the state legislature’s own text.

Our view The owner, in the owner’s words

Members of Congress are bound by their oath and the Constitution to represent the people. Both parties took control from the people while ordinary Americans were busy caring for their families. We the people own and operate this country. Another election just puts more people in to do the same thing. An Article V convention of states is the only way to change Congress.
