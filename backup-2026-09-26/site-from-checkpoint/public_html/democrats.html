SNAP was slashed for billionaires On the record

The claim

Republicans slashed SNAP to pay for tax breaks for billionaires. Millions of children do not know where the next meal is.

The record

Public Law 119-21 changed a work rule. The Congressional Budget Office says able-bodied adults through age 64, and adults whose children are 14 or older, can draw benefits for three months in three years unless they work or train about 80 hours a month. The estimate is about 2.4 million fewer people in an average month. That is not a line that takes food from millions of children and hands it to billionaires. The work rule is the fact the post left out.

The Court found election interference On the record

The claim

The Supreme Court rebuked Trump’s election interference and blocked restrictions that would make it harder to vote.

The record

The September order is an emergency stay, docket 26A305, on a Postal Service rule, issued before the midterms. It is not a judgment that the President committed election interference. On June 29, 2026, in Watson v. Republican National Committee, the Court read the election-day statutes, 2 U.S.C. §§ 1 and 7 and 3 U.S.C. § 1. Those statutes do not say when a mailed ballot must arrive. A reading of a statute is not a finding that the law was broken.

Stop the second segment On the record

The claim

The hallway clip was a lie. He was a political prisoner. Order the host to stop, and keep the rest of the tape in the House.

The record

The hallway minute is real. The plea came in September 2021, before the broadcast. Judge Lamberth later called the aired videos decidedly not exculpatory. The U.S. Attorney’s tally at forty-eight months: about 1,583 charged, about 1,009 guilty pleas, about 667 sentenced to incarceration, about 608 charged with assaulting officers, zero charged under 18 U.S.C. § 2383. Those pleas were taken while the public did not have the archive. Speaker Johnson said about 5 percent of the hours would stay back for security. The uncensored hours were offered in a viewing room inside the Capitol. The country could not travel there. The website stayed a fraction. On March 7, 2023, Majority Leader Schumer told Rupert Murdoch, from the Senate floor, to stop a second segment. A pardon on January 20, 2025, is not a finding that the pleas were forced. The Speaker’s files: the House Administration Subcommittee channel on Rumble. www.stophate.com is a private site, not that archive.
