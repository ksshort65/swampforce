## The hospital. Then the morgue.

- Slug: the-hospital-and-the-morgue
- Series: The Border
- Date: 2026-09-20

**Our view:** Emergency rooms filled, then Americans died. The names of the accused are on ICE letterhead.

A hospital that takes Medicare already has to stabilize whoever walks in. That is [42 U.S.C. § 1395dd](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/1395dd) — EMTALA. It does not ask for a Social Security number at the door. The American who paid the premiums waits behind the person CBP just released. The statute that was supposed to stop the bill is [8 U.S.C. § 1611](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/8/1611). The workaround is emergency Medicaid.

[CBO, October 2, 2024](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/60805), answering House Budget: from fiscal 2017 through 2023, federal and state governments spent about $27 billion on emergency Medicaid for people ineligible for full Medicaid because of immigration status. CBO’s table peaks in fiscal 2021: $7.05 billion, then $5.40 billion in 2022 and $3.78 billion in 2023, against $1.5 billion to $3.1 billion a year in 2017–2020. Two caveats CBO prints itself: Washington raised the federal matching rate from January 2020 through December 2023, and CBO cannot say how much of the money went to people here illegally rather than to lawful noncitizens still in their waiting period. Fiscal 2021 also began in October 2020, under the prior administration. That is not a clinic visit. That is an ER that was already short of beds.

> **Our view:** The American who paid FICA waited. The statute had already said who the check was for.

### Then some of them killed people

Not a mood. Names. Laken Riley, a Georgia nursing student, was killed by Jose Antonio Ibarra, a Venezuelan illegal alien. [DHS](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/01/29/dhs-celebrates-one-year-laken-riley-act) says he was a Tren de Aragua member, was released into the country in September 2022, was later arrested by NYPD for acting in a manner to injure a child, and was released again. Then Laken was dead. Congress named a detention statute after her. DHS says that in the first year of that Act, ICE arrested more than 21,400 illegal aliens with the crimes the Act lists.

Rachel Morin, a Maryland mother of five, was killed in August 2023. [ICE](https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/hsi-baltimore-lends-vital-assist-local-partners-investigation-suspected-killer): Victor Martinez-Hernandez, a Salvadoran national unlawfully present in the United States, was arrested in Tulsa on June 14, 2024. The [Harford County State’s Attorney](https://www.harfordcountystatesattorney.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/VMH-PR-Sentenicng-8-11-25.pdf) secured his conviction; he was sentenced to life without parole in August 2025.

[ICE’s FY2024 Annual Report](https://www.ice.gov/doclib/eoy/iceAnnualReportFY2024.pdf): the 81,312 criminal noncitizens ERO arrested that year carried 516,050 charges and convictions. In that pile: 2,894 homicides. 5,001 damage to property. 18,579 sexual assault and sex offenses. 2,766 kidnappings. That is not “immigrants commit crime.” That is ICE counting the people it arrested who already had those charges or convictions.

[ICE, in a signed letter to Rep. Tony Gonzales](https://homeland.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/24-01143-ICEs-Signed-Response-to-Representative-Tony-Gonzales.pdf): as of July 21, 2024, about 647,600 noncitizens with criminal convictions or pending criminal charges were on ICE’s non-detained docket. Not in custody. [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats/cbp-enforcement-statistics/criminal-noncitizen-statistics-fy2024) homicide and manslaughter convictions among Border Patrol criminal-alien arrests: three, three, two, three in FY2017–20. Then 60, 62, 29, 29 in FY2021–24. The door and the rap sheet rose together.

**Our view:** Defund ICE is a vote to keep that docket on the street. [The hospital bill](https://www.cbo.gov/publication/60805) is already in CMS. The names are already on [ICE letterhead](https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/operation-angels-honor-14-day-nationwide-ice-operation-honor-laken-riley-results-more). The party that opened the door still wants the agency that picks the killers up taken off the payroll. That is the record. Not a feeling.
