## The noise.

- Slug: the-noise
- Series: Congress
- Date: 2026-09-02

**Our view:** Congress talks for a living. The country still does not get a budget. Words without a ledger are the job they invented to keep the job.

**Our view:** They do not fail at talking. That is the only muscle they kept. Hearings that are not hearings. Floor speeches that never become a vote. A Sunday show that names a villain and never names a line in a statute. That is a shop that stopped delivering the thing it was hired to deliver.

> **Our view:** If it will not fit on a slide with a dollar sign, it is not a plan. It is noise.

The 119th still owes twelve money bills by October 1 — a deadline Congress has not met on time for every bill since fiscal 1997, per the [Congressional Research Service](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12324). They will say the other jersey did it. Both jerseys took the oath. Both jerseys took the $7.258 billion. The country still does not get a budget it can read.

This series is the hearing they will not schedule. Next: they sold the split so neighbor would fight neighbor instead of reading the annex. Then: full time or go home. Then: the whole bill — every page, every reconciliation print, posted before the gavel, or they do not get to vote. The mic is not the job.
