## They opened the border.

- Slug: they-opened-the-border
- Series: The Border
- Date: 2026-09-20

**Our view:** Millions of encounters. Fentanyl in the morgue. Hotels on the taxpayer. The door was a policy. The policy was Democratic.

The southwest border is not a mystery. [U.S. Customs and Border Protection](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats/southwest-land-border-encounters) publishes the encounters. U.S. Border Patrol’s count at the Mexico line, from CBP’s own fiscal-year tables from [CBP’s own fiscal-year tables](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats/southwest-land-border-encounters): fiscal 2021, 1.66 million. Fiscal 2022, 2.21 million. Fiscal 2023, 2.05 million. Fiscal 2024, 1.53 million. That is more than seven million encounters in four fiscal years (fiscal 2021 began in October 2020, under the prior administration; the rest fell under this one). Some people are counted more than once. The pile is still the pile. Fiscal 2025, after the Oval changed: 237,538. The lowest since 1970. A door that can close that fast was a door that had been held open.

The Rio Grande was not the only door. [CBP nationwide](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats/cbp-enforcement-statistics): 1.96 million in FY2021, 2.77 million in FY2022, 3.20 million in FY2023, 2.90 million in FY2024. That is 10.83 million encounters in four years — southwest land, the northern line, airports, seaports, Miami and every other sector CBP counts. [DHS OHSS](https://ohss.dhs.gov/khsm/cbp-encounters) splits it: 8.72 million on southwest land, about 2.10 million on the rest of the map. FY2025 nationwide fell to 691,906.

> **Our view:** A door that falls shut in a year was a policy, not weather.

Who held the gavel when it opened: Democrats ran the White House from January 2021 to January 2025. They ran both the House and the Senate from January 2021 to January 2023. They ended Remain in Mexico. They ended the Title 42 public-health expulsion on May 11, 2023. They ran mass parole programs that turned a crossing into a lawful stay and a work permit. [SSI is not Social Security](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi/spotlights/spot-non-citizens.htm). It is general revenue. SSA’s own spotlight lists refugees and asylees as eligible categories; a parolee qualifies only in narrow cases, such as a parolee who has held that status for years and meets other conditions. Parole alone does not open the check. [8 U.S.C. § 1611](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/8/1611) already barred most federal benefits for aliens who are not qualified. [What the taxpayer bought](/dispatch/what-the-taxpayer-bought) is the rest of that stack: cash cards, phones, clothing, housing, property damage, and the homicide docket.

### What every household paid

Fentanyl. [CDC / NCHS](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/blog/posts/2026/03/most-common-drugs-in-u-s-overdose-deaths-2017-2023.html): fentanyl was the leading drug in overdose deaths every year from 2017 through 2023. Deaths involving fentanyl rose from 27,542 in 2017 to 73,944 in 2022. That is not a panel. That is a morgue table. Schools, first responders, and parents paid in funerals.

Children. [DHS](https://www.dhs.gov/news/2026/02/24/making-america-safe-again-state-dhs-under-president-trump-and-secretary-noem) says the prior administration lost track of more than 450,000 unaccompanied children, and that, as of February 2026, its search had located 145,000. Those are DHS’s figures. The independent record is the inspector general: [DHS OIG-24-46](https://www.oig.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/assets/2024-08/OIG-24-46-Aug24.pdf) found ICE could not monitor the locations of all released unaccompanied children, that more than 32,000 failed to appear for court, and that about 291,000 had not been served a Notice to Appear. Sex trafficking of children is already [18 U.S.C. § 1591](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1591). The open door fed the crime.
