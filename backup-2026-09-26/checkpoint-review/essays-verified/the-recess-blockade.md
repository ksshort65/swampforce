## The recess blockade

- Slug: the-recess-blockade
- Series: Congress
- Date: 2026-08-23

**Our view:** Pro forma gavels. Fake sessions. A president who cannot appoint. Article II, gutted on purpose.

Clinton: 139 recess appointments. Bush: 171. Obama: 32 until the Court stripped the tactic. Trump first term: 0. Biden: 0 — the Senate was aligned. Trump now: 0 as of this writing, because someone still walks into an empty chamber every few days, bangs a gavel, and leaves.

[NLRB v. Noel Canning (2014)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supct/pdf/12-1281.pdf) held that a recess of fewer than ten days is presumptively too short to trigger the Recess Appointments Clause, and that the Senate is in session when it says it is, if it keeps the ability to do business. Armed with that, a faction invented fake sessions — no business, one politician, a few seconds. Fiction with a gavel. It turns off [Article II, Section 2, Clause 3](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/article-2/) on purpose so a president the country just elected cannot staff the government.

**Our view:** The Framers wrote the Recess Appointments Clause as a check against a Senate that would not act. Fake sessions are a check against the voters. That is the 2024 mandate dying in an empty room. Track the calendar. Count the days between gavels. The numbers are the argument.
