The verified text, checked against primary records on Sep 24, 2026. Passages marked Our view are opinion.

Our view DHS says it has located 145,000 of the unaccompanied children it says the prior administration lost track of. This journal stands with the search.

This journal treats missing children as a search, not as a campaign costume. The Department of Homeland Security stated in February 2026 that the prior administration lost more than 450,000 unaccompanied children at the southwest border, and that a joint DHS and HHS effort had located 145,000 of them. DHS says the search for the rest continues. Those are DHS’s own figures; the inspector general’s audit below is the independent record.

Our view Find them. All of them. The search belongs to the agencies charged with the children, not to a protest line at the door.

The numbers have to be named as the government named them. The DHS Office of Inspector General reported in August 2024 (OIG-24-46) that ICE transferred more than 448,000 unaccompanied children to HHS from fiscal years 2019 through 2023, that more than 32,000 of those children failed to appear for immigration court, and that as of May 2024 ICE had not served a Notice to Appear on more than 291,000 of them. That is an accounting failure written by the government’s own inspector, not by a panel.

HHS publishes current counts of children still in Office of Refugee Resettlement care on its unaccompanied children data page. Those monthly tables are the live inventory. They are not a complete map of every child already released to a sponsor. The inspector general’s file and the HHS tables have to be read together. One is the pipeline. The other is who is still in a bed tonight.

What the statutes already say

Sex trafficking of children is already a federal felony under 18 U.S.C. § 1591. Forced labor is already a federal felony under 18 U.S.C. § 1589. Bringing in and harboring certain aliens is already a federal felony under 8 U.S.C. § 1324. Homeland Security Investigations exists to knock on a door when a report sits in a drawer. If a public official demands that ICE be kept off that door, the official is not confused about the statute. The official is choosing that the report not be worked.

Our view This page will not describe a child’s body to score a point. The file is ugly enough in nouns the Code already uses: debt bondage to a smuggler, labor that is not a summer job, sexual violence named as the crime it is. The remedy is the search, the warrant, the home visit, and the court date. The remedy is not a slogan.

Our view It is a demand that every name in that file be found, and that every official who blocks the finding be removed from the work by the voters and by the law.
