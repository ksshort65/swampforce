Our view Pause the clip. Name the job. Read down the names.

The country was given a clip instead of a class. A network needs anger in six seconds. A teacher needs the sentence finished. This journal is the second thing.

Here is the whole method. When a caption names a president, ask one question: what did he do on this one job? Not his personality. The job. The border is a job. War is a job. Factories are a job. Politicians talk about jobs. Builders close them or they don’t.

We will do the border together

Clinton wrote a tough immigration law and still saw roughly 1 million to 1.6 million Border Patrol apprehensions a year at the southwest border. Bush hired more agents and passed a fence bill. The hole stayed. Obama let families go and wrote DACA without Congress. Trump’s first term put people in Mexico to wait; numbers fell. Biden’s years: more than two million in fiscal 2022 and again in 2023, and many were released into the country. This term: the lowest crossings in fifty years, and a new law that keeps criminal illegal immigrants locked up.

Our view That was a class. A party was not required. Six names and one job were. The boxes below are two more jobs — war, and the factories — written the same way. Last line first. That is today. Then read up. That is who opened the wound.

Our view If a friend only has six seconds, do not send them this whole page. Send them one sentence: Biden opened the border. This term shut it. Then send the link. That is teaching. A longer caption is still a caption.
