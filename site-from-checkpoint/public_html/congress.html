On the record

The Speech or Debate Clause (art. I, § 6) protects legislative acts. It did not cover a senator's private publication of the Pentagon Papers (Gravel v. United States, 1972) or a senator's newsletters and press release (Hutchinson v. Proxmire, 1979). Each House may punish its members and, with a two-thirds vote, expel one (art. I, § 5); the House has expelled 6 members and censured 29 in its history. On June 21, 2023, the House censured Rep. Adam Schiff (H. Res. 521), 213–209, with 6 voting present.

Our view · Opinion

Where are the adults in the room? Congress was hired to manage this country's money and watch over every federal program. Instead, too many of its members spend their time blaming whoever sits in the White House for problems that Congress was supposed to catch. While they fight each other, the debt grows, the fraud spreads, and the country gets more divided. We need them to stop acting like children and start acting like the people we sent to Washington to represent us.
