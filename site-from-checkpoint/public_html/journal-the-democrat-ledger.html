SNAP was slashed for billionaires

They ran: Republicans slashed SNAP to pay for tax breaks for billionaires. Millions of children do not know where the next meal is.

The file: Public Law 119-21 changed a work rule. The Congressional Budget Office says able-bodied adults through age 64, and adults whose children are 14 or older, can draw benefits for three months in three years unless they work or train about 80 hours a month. The estimate is about 2.4 million fewer people in an average month. That is not a line that takes food from millions of children and hands it to billionaires. The work rule is the fact the post left out.

Record: cbo.gov ↗

The Court found election interference

They ran: The Supreme Court rebuked Trump’s election interference and blocked restrictions that would make it harder to vote.

The file: The September order is an emergency stay, docket 26A305, on a Postal Service rule, issued before the midterms. It is not a judgment that the President committed election interference. On June 29, 2026, in Watson v. Republican National Committee, the Court read the election-day statutes, 2 U.S.C. §§ 1 and 7 and 3 U.S.C. § 1. Those statutes do not say when a mailed ballot must arrive. A reading of a statute is not a finding that the law was broken.

Record: supremecourt.gov ↗
