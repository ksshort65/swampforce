Our view Millions of encounters. Fentanyl in the morgue. Hotels on the taxpayer. The door was a policy. The policy was Democratic.

The southwest border is not a mystery. U.S. Customs and Border Protection publishes the encounters. U.S. Border Patrol’s count at the Mexico line, from CBP’s own fiscal-year tables from CBP’s own fiscal-year tables: fiscal 2021, 1.66 million. Fiscal 2022, 2.21 million. Fiscal 2023, 2.05 million. Fiscal 2024, 1.53 million. That is more than seven million encounters in four fiscal years (fiscal 2021 began in October 2020, under the prior administration; the rest fell under this one). Some people are counted more than once. The pile is still the pile. Fiscal 2025, after the Oval changed: 237,538. The lowest since 1970. A door that can close that fast was a door that had been held open.

The Rio Grande was not the only door. CBP nationwide: 1.96 million in FY2021, 2.77 million in FY2022, 3.20 million in FY2023, 2.90 million in FY2024. That is 10.83 million encounters in four years — southwest land, the northern line, airports, seaports, Miami and every other sector CBP counts. DHS OHSS splits it: 8.73 million on southwest land, about 2.10 million on the rest of the map. FY2025 nationwide fell to 691,906.

Image: /images/chart-border-toll.jpg — What Americans still pay — encounters, hospitals, hotels, fentanyl, missing children, the criminal docket

Then they were moved, and the cities paid. New York City’s Comptroller booked the rooms: $1.41 billion in FY2023, $3.70 billion in FY2024, $3.02 billion in FY2025 — $8.13 billion in one city. DHS OIG: FEMA awarded $1.4 billion in Shelter and Services and EFSP-H in FY2023–24, money transferred from CBP.

Our view A door that falls shut in a year was a policy, not weather.

Who held the gavel when it opened: Democrats ran the White House from January 2021 to January 2025. They ran both the House and the Senate from January 2021 to January 2023. They ended Remain in Mexico. They ended the Title 42 public-health expulsion on May 11, 2023. They ran mass parole programs that turned a crossing into a lawful stay and a work permit. SSI is not Social Security. It is general revenue. SSA’s own spotlight lists refugees and asylees as eligible categories; a parolee qualifies only in narrow cases, such as a parolee who has held that status for years and meets other conditions. Parole alone does not open the check. 8 U.S.C. § 1611 already barred most federal benefits for aliens who are not qualified. What the taxpayer bought is the rest of that stack: cash cards, phones, clothing, housing, property damage, and the homicide docket.

February 24, 2026. Joint session. The President asked every legislator to stand if they agreed with this sentence: the first duty of the American government is to protect American citizens, not illegal aliens. C-SPAN recorded the ask. The full address is on the same tape. Watch who stands.

Our view The First Amendment protects peaceable assembly. It does not protect doxxing an ICE officer, assaulting him, or smashing the building. 18 U.S.C. § 111 is assault on a federal officer. 18 U.S.C. § 119 is publishing his home address to threaten him. 18 U.S.C. § 1361 is government property. DHS: 275 assaults on ICE officers from January 20 through December 31, 2025, against 19 in the same stretch of 2024. 66 vehicular attacks against 2. DOJ: Prairieland Detention Center, July 4, 2025 — an Antifa cell sentenced for attacking the facility. Minnesota: 15 indicted for assaulting federal officers and destroying government property. Sanctuary rhetoric is in the DHS file. That is unrest. It is not a rally.

What every household paid

Fentanyl. CDC / NCHS: fentanyl was the leading drug in overdose deaths every year from 2017 through 2023. Deaths involving fentanyl rose from 27,542 in 2017 to 73,944 in 2022. That is not a panel. That is a morgue table. Schools, first responders, and parents paid in funerals.

Children. DHS says the prior administration lost track of more than 450,000 unaccompanied children, and that its search has located 145,000. Those are DHS’s figures. The independent record is the inspector general: DHS OIG-24-46 found ICE could not monitor the locations of all released unaccompanied children, that more than 32,000 failed to appear for court, and that about 291,000 had not been served a Notice to Appear. Sex trafficking of children is already 18 U.S.C. § 1591. The open door fed the crime.

Our view Wages, rents, emergency rooms, and hotel bills. Schools added bodies without adding buildings. The American who waited in line, paid FICA, and followed the statute watched the line collapse. That is not xenophobia. That is a queue that stopped meaning anything.

Our view This disaster has a party. Democrats held the Oval and, for two years, both chambers, while Border Patrol’s southwest count ran past two million in fiscal 2022 and 2023. Republicans who voted to keep the door shut were not the authors of the parole memos. When the Oval changed, the number fell to a fifty-year low. CBP counted the door. CDC counted the morgue. The inspector general counted the children without a Notice to Appear. That is the record of a door, and of who held it open.
