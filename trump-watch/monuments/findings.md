## 2. Funding by project (primary records)

### White House ballroom ("East Wing Modernization Project")
- **Managed by:** the White House complex. The WH statement of Jul 31, 2025 names the NPS, the White House Military Office and the USSS as planning partners; NCPC approved the plans on Apr 2, 2026 (file 8733, cited in the bill text).
- **Construction:** according to the WH (Jul 31, 2025), "President Trump, and other patriot donors, have generously committed to donating the funds necessary to build this approximately $200 million dollar structure. The United States Secret Service will provide the necessary security enhancements." Source: https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2025/07/the-white-house-announces-white-house-ballroom-construction-to-begin/
- **Reconciliation text** (Senate Judiciary title, draft MDM26A10, May 2026), Sec. 1115: $1,000,000,000 to USSS "for the purposes of security adjustments and upgrades, including within the perimeter fence of the White House Compound ... relating to the East Wing Modernization Project". (b) LIMITATION: "None of the funds ... may be used for non-security elements of the East Wing Modernization Project." The provision was dropped after the parliamentarian's ruling, and the final bill did not include it (REPORTED: AP, ABC). Text copy: raw/sjc-recon.pdf, from https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/reconciliation_-_senate_judiciary_committee_title.pdf
- **OMB apportionment** (USSS PC&I, TAFS 070-0401 2025/2029), iterations of Jun 12 and Jun 15, 2026: line "Category B -- White House Security Measures" = $340.8M, then +$45M = $385.8M. The footnote cites the P.L. 119-21 sec. 100057 spend plan. The O&S account adds about $10.75M, for about $396.6M in total. Mirror: https://openomb.org/file/11525117 (it links the primary on apportionment-public.max.gov).
- **Open question:** the Senate Appropriations Democrats' letter of Jun 22, 2026 asks GAO whether this use is lawful: https://www.appropriations.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/260622_letter_to_gao_on_usss_funding_for_ballroompdf.pdf. No GAO decision was located.
- **REPORTED:**
  - The WaPo reports that the contractor estimated a cost above $600M and that more than half of the funding would come from government entities.
  - The Oct 23, 2025 donor list (37 names, no amounts).
  - Trump's first 2025 paycheck went to the White House Historical Association (his Truth Social post of Aug 6, 2025, as reported; no amount).

### Triumphal Arch (Memorial Circle)
- **Managed by:** NPS, George Washington Memorial Parkway. NPS issued the EA/FONSI (June 2026; "not a final decision") and a Section 106 assessment (revised Aug 28, 2026): https://parkplanning.nps.gov/document.cfm?documentID=151577

## 4. Taliban: U.S. money since Aug 2021
- **SIGAR 24-22 (May 2024):**
  - 38 of 65 responding implementing partners paid at least **$10.9M** to the Taliban-controlled government: $10.4M in taxes, $346,839 in utilities, $176,596 in fees, $9,215 in customs.
  - The U.S. provided more than $2.8B in assistance since Aug 2021.
  - SIGAR calls this "likely only a fraction," because UN agencies did not report subawardee payments.
  - Report: https://reliefweb.int/report/afghanistan/sigar-24-22-audit-report-us-funds-benefitting-taliban-controlled-government-implementing-partners-paid-least-109-million-and-were-pressured-divert-assistance (the sigar.mil page returns a 500 error from here; the mirror PDF is saved at raw/sigar-2422.pdf)
- **SIGAR 24-12 (Jan 2024):** the UN "purchased and transported more than $2.9 billion" in cash to Afghanistan. This is UN cash bought with donor funds; it is not a U.S.-only figure. https://reliefweb.int/report/afghanistan/sigar-24-12-evaluation-report-cash-shipments-afghanistan-un-has-purchased-and-transported-more-29-billion-afghanistan-implement-humanitarian-assistance-january-2024
- **SIGAR final quarterly report (68th, Jul 30, 2025):**
  - "In 2025, the United States terminated all foreign assistance awards with activities in Afghanistan." By Apr 8, 2025, all awards had been deemed not aligned (EO 14169).
  - State ended emergency food aid "to mitigate Taliban interference."
  - The Taliban seized U.S.-funded assets during closeouts (e.g., $42,000 of BHA items); 56 armored vehicles were at risk.
  - The same report states that the UN cash shipments "benefit the Taliban" (SIGAR 24-32-IP).
  - Report: raw/qr2025-07.pdf; https://www.sigar.mil/Portals/147/Files/Reports/Quarterly-Reports/2025-07-30qr.pdf
- **SIGAR Final Report (Dec 2025):**
  - "SIGAR will close its doors on January 31, 2026, pursuant to Section 7809 of the NDAA for Fiscal Year 2025."
  - It also records DOD's estimate of about $7.1B in equipment left to the ANDSF, now under Taliban control. raw/sigar-final.pdf
- **CRs and lapses:** a continuing resolution extends funding, generally at the prior year's rate and with prior conditions (CRS R46595: https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R46595). A lapse stops new obligations. **None of the SIGAR records above links the Taliban payments or the cash shipments to a CR or a lapse.** The payments happened under whatever appropriations funded the awards. The record does not connect the two issues.

## 5. Ratings
- **Ballroom:**
  - Oct 23, 2025 "vanity projects ... ego": **Opinion**.
  - May 12/20, 2026 "reconciliation bill that would force Americans to pay for Trump's billion-dollar ballroom" / "flagrant, shameless lying": **Rated misleading.** The $1B in the text was for USSS security and expressly barred non-security ballroom elements; it was dropped; construction is pledged as private. Context: in June 2026 OMB set aside about $396.6M in existing USSS funds for "White House Security Measures", and a GAO review was requested.
- **Reflecting Pool:** "no-bid contract ... at the taxpayer's expense": **Consistent with the record.** The main $14.65M contract and the $1.84M contract were not competed. The money came from NPS federal accounts: visitor-fee receipts, the construction appropriation, and $5M from the NPS donations account. The "personal pool guy" part is REPORTED, not checked.
- **Arch:** named as a vanity project: **Opinion.** Funding fact: $15M in NEH funds is reserved for it (primary).
