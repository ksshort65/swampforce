# Trump Accountability Baseline (compiled Sep 24, 2026)

**Standard:** The same one used for movement-watch. Facts come only from primary records: OGE filings, whitehouse.gov, DOJ, SEC, FCC, GAO, court rulings and company releases. News is a lead only, and anything news-only is marked *reported, not confirmed by primary record*.

This document does not conclude whether anyone is "corrupt." It lists documented overlaps and whether any official body has made a finding. See `log.csv` and `conflicts.csv`.

## 1. White House visitor logs
- **Not published.** [whitehouse.gov/disclosures](https://www.whitehouse.gov/disclosures/) posts ethics waivers, financial disclosures and transaction reports but **no visitor logs**, and `/disclosures/visitor-logs/` returns 404.
- The Biden White House ([archive](https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/disclosures/visitor-logs/)) and the Obama White House ([archive](https://obamawhitehouse.archives.gov/briefing-room/disclosures/visitor-records)) published logs voluntarily.
- **2017:** the White House announced on Apr 14, 2017 that it would end voluntary release, citing "grave national security risks and privacy concerns." This is *reported*; no archived official page was found.
- **Court record:** in [Doyle v. DHS](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/18-2814/18-2814-2020-05-18.html) (2d Cir., May 18, 2020), the court held that White House and Mar-a-Lago visitor logs held by the Secret Service "are not agency records subject to FOIA" ([DOJ OIP summary](https://www.justice.gov/oip/doyle-v-dhs-no-18-2814-2020-wl-2516644-2d-cir-may-18-2020-lohier-j)). In practice, the public has no legal route to these logs unless the White House releases them voluntarily.
- No 2025–26 official statement or new lawsuit was found.

  - Mar 6, 2025 [Strategic Bitcoin Reserve EO](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/03/establishment-of-the-strategic-bitcoin-reserve-and-united-states-digital-asset-stockpile/).

  - Abu Dhabi's MGX invested $2B in Binance ([release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mgx-backs-binance-in-landmark-investment-302400050.html)). That the deal used USD1 is *reported*.

  - Trump pardoned Binance founder Changpeng Zhao on Oct 21, 2025.

  - The Pentagon accepted a Qatari 747-8i as the bridge Air Force One. The [Air Force](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4474728/vc-25b-bridge-program-completes-flight-testing-prepares-for-summer-rollout/) confirms the acceptance; the May 2025 date is *reported*. - **Trump v. IRS:** DOJ settled the President's own lawsuit by creating a [$1.776B "Anti-Weaponization Fund"](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-announces-anti-weaponization-fund). No money went to the Trump family, and the President can remove the fund's members.

## 5. Official findings
- **GAO:** found Impoundment Control Act violations (Head Start, NIH, FEMA, DOE; e.g. [B-337202](https://www.gao.gov/assets/890/880226.pdf)). These are budget-law findings, not ethics findings.
- **Inspectors general:** a federal judge ruled the firing of 17 IGs unlawful but did not reinstate them ([ruling](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.277385/gov.uscourts.dcd.277385.54.0_2.pdf)).
- **Emoluments:** no court has ruled on the merits for 2025–26. First-term cases ended on standing or mootness.
- **Office of Special Counsel:** no Hatch Act finding against senior 2025–26 officials was found. In 2021 OSC found that 13 first-term officials violated the Hatch Act ([report](https://www.osc.gov/news/2021-11-09/osc-issues-hatch-act-report-documenting-violations-by-13-senior-trump-administration-officials-including-at-the-2020-republican-national-convention)).
- **Bottom line:** every overlap in `conflicts.csv` has **no official finding**. That means no court, IG, GAO or OSC has found a conflict-of-interest or emoluments violation. It does not mean no overlap exists.
