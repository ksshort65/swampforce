# Open questions: not confirmed from a primary source (kept OUT of section-draft.md)

Each item below lists what is claimed, why it is not in the draft, and the primary source that would settle it.

## George Floyd / officers
1. **Dr. Fowler's manner-of-death opinion ("undetermined").** News accounts say he would have ruled the manner "undetermined." No primary text was found saying this. Judge Cahill's findings do not quote it. **Needed:** Chauvin trial transcript, April 14, 2021, around pp. 5446–5617.
2. **Baker testimony on the fentanyl level being "fatal."** Chauvin's appellate brief says Baker testified the level was "three times the amount considered fatal" (TT-4926–4929), but that is the defense's characterization. The draft uses only the court's findings and the prosecutor's memo. **Needed:** transcript pages 4926–4932.
3. **The state carbon monoxide test.** Chauvin's brief says the State disclosed a 1.5% carbon monoxide blood-gas result on April 15, 2021. Judge Cahill's findings record Baker testifying that the level was "normal." **Needed:** the trial exhibit or transcript.
4. **Imposed state sentences for Lane, Kueng and Thao.** News reports give Lane 36 months (Sept. 21, 2022), Kueng 42 months (Dec. 9, 2022) and Thao 57 months (Aug. 7, 2023). Only the plea documents, which carry recommended terms, and the Thao verdict were retrieved. **Needed:** sentencing orders from MCRO or mncourts.gov high-profile case pages, or MN AG releases on those dates.
5. **Chauvin's § 2255 motion** (D. Minn. 0:21-cr-00108, Doc. 544; U.S. response Doc. 553; discovery order Doc. 565). The final disposition is unknown. Also, the Eighth Circuit opinion affirming Thao (No. 22-2701, Aug. 2023) returned 404 at the guessed ca8 URL.
6. **The May 6, 2019 arrest itself.** No charging document or disposition was retrieved. The only record used is Judge Cahill's footnote on testimony about it. Neither the pill-related facts reported in news nor the trial-court order limiting that evidence (Dkt-422 per the defense brief) were retrieved.
7. **Private autopsy by Drs. Baden and Wilson (asphyxia).** The only record found is the family attorneys' summary (Exhibit 2, State v. Thao). That is a party statement, so it was left out of the draft.
8. **2020 Chauvin charging complaint and the earlier third-degree murder/manslaughter charging decisions.** Not retrieved.
