# Documented conflicts or improprieties (State v. Chauvin and related cases)

This is copied from floyd-deep.md. Sources are in sources.csv.

- **One documented ruling.** Judge Cahill's Nov. 4, 2020 order limited Hennepin County Attorney Freeman and assistants LeFevour, Sweasy and Lofton. They could not act as trial advocates or sign pleadings, because of an advocate-witness problem created by their May 27, 2020 meeting with Dr. Baker without a non-attorney witness. The court called this "careless." It is not a finding of bias, dishonesty or evidence tampering.
- **One judicial rebuke on the record (not a ruling against a party).** Judge Cahill called officials' public comments on the case during trial "abhorrent," referring to Rep. Waters, and denied a mistrial.
- **One appellate acknowledgment.** The Court of Appeals said the police chief's and public-safety commissioner's pre-trial "murder" statements "presupposed guilt," but held that any prejudice was cured.
- **No other conflicts or improprieties were found in the primary records reviewed.** There was no recusal motion against either judge, no finding of bias by any court, no finding of juror misconduct (juror 52's claim was rejected on the record), and no finding of prosecutorial misconduct (the June 2021 new-trial order found none). Campaign contributions, prior employment and appointments listed in `ties.csv` are ordinary ties with no court finding attached.
