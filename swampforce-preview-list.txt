HOW TO PUT THIS ON NAMECHEAP
- Upload everything in this zip (including the hidden .htaccess file) into the domain's top folder (public_html), or into the top folder of a subdomain.
- It will NOT work inside a subfolder like yoursite.com/preview/, because every page asks for its files starting from the domain root (/assets, /images).
- The .htaccess file lets links like /betrayal, /topics and /scorecard open directly, and sends unknown addresses to the site's own "Not Found" page.
- The zip is about 68 MB, over the 25 MB goal. Almost all of that is pictures and documents (images 47 MB, ethics-docs 13 MB, old-site 7.5 MB, lawfare-docs 4.8 MB, impeachment-docs 2.6 MB). Nothing was removed to make it smaller.
