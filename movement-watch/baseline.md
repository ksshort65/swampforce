## 1. Abdul El-Sayed
**Who:** Physician and former Detroit health director. Ran for Michigan governor in 2018. He is now the **Democratic nominee for US Senate in Michigan** and faces Republican Mike Rogers (FEC S4MI00595) on Nov 3, 2026.

**Primary result (Aug 4, 2026):** El-Sayed 744,952 (48.45%), Haley Stevens 731,160 (47.55%), Mallory McMorrow 61,516 (4.00%). The Board of State Canvassers certified the result on Aug 24, 2026 ([minutes](https://www.michigan.gov/sos/-/media/Project/Websites/sos/BSC-Meeting-Minutes/2026/August-24-2026-BSC-draft-minutes.pdf)). FEC candidate ID: [S6MI00418](https://www.fec.gov/data/candidate/S6MI00418/).

**Positions in his own words (campaign site):**
- [Medicare for All](https://abdulforsenate.com/priority/medicare-for-all-the-path-to-a-healthier-america/): "extend it to every single American from cradle to grave without premiums, copays, or deductibles."
- [Taxes](https://abdulforsenate.com/priority/money-in-your-pocket/): tax capital gains over $1M as ordinary income, a billionaire wealth tax, and lift the Social Security cap.
- [Money out of politics / foreign policy](https://abdulforsenate.com/priority/money-out-of-politics/):
  - Abolish ICE ("It must be abolished"). End the filibuster. Ban super PACs.
  - Opposes the "illegal and unjustifiable war in Iran."
  - Says US weapons are used "to perpetrate a genocide in Gaza" and backs "an immediate arms embargo on Israel."
  - Supports aid to Ukraine and funding for UNRWA.

**DSA relationship:**
- DSA's own 2019 article says he shared the stage with Rep. Tlaib at the Detroit DSA Douglass-Debs dinner ([primary](https://www.dsausa.org/blog/chapter-and-verse-how-to-raise-funds-and-build-community/)).
- Five other appearances (2017–2020) are *reported, not confirmed by primary record* (Free Beacon via Fox).
- His reported 2026 denials: "I'm not technically or practically DSA" and "I believe in capitalism." These are *reported*.
- DSA's national electoral site shows **no national DSA endorsement** of him.

**2026 timeline:**
- Apr 7: rallies at MSU and UM with Hasan Piker, Rep. Tlaib and Rep. Summer Lee ([rally video](https://www.detroitnews.com/videos/media/video/2026/04/07/listen-to-abdul-el-sayed-hasan-piker-rashida-tlaib-and-summer-lee-at-michigan-rally/89510348007/)).
- May 3: Detroit rally with Sen. Sanders (*reported*).
- Jul 7 and Jul 17: CNN KFile and Bridge Michigan fact-check his defund-the-police record.
- Aug 4: wins the primary. Outside spending against him (AIPAC-linked UDP, more than $15M) is *reported*; it can be checked in FEC independent-expenditure data.
- Aug 24: [statement](https://abdulforsenate.com/2026/08/statement-from-dr-abdul-el-sayed-on-hasan-pikers-comments-regarding-jeremy-moss/) distancing himself from Piker's remarks: "Antisemitism is a scourge… nobody speaks for this campaign besides me and my campaign spokespeople."

**Flagged claim:** his statement "I actually never, never called for defunding" is misleading when set against his [June 2020 WDET interview](https://wdet.org/2020/06/23/former-gubernatorial-candidate-abdul-el-sayed-on-policing-and-public-health/). See `candidates.csv`.

## 2. Hasan Piker
**Who:** Los Angeles-based Twitch streamer (HasanAbi) and self-described democratic socialist. **His formal DSA membership is not confirmed**, so he is not labeled a member here. In late August 2026, DSA's X account reportedly called him "our Twitch streamer" (*reported*).

**In his own words:** In the [Axios Show interview (full video, Sep 18, 2026)](https://youtu.be/hBJx9ypjF90), he said:
- The US is worse than al-Qaeda "pound for pound, as far as the number of deaths."
- He apologized for his 2019 "America deserved 9/11" remark.
- Charlie Kirk's assassination was "unjustifiable."
- Political violence is not justified, with a caveat about occupied populations.
- He would support AOC in 2028.

These quotes are relayed from coverage; check exact wording against the video.

**2026 timeline:**

- Jul 31, Aug 3 and Aug 4: at El-Sayed canvass and election-night events (*reported*).
- Sep 18: Axios interview.

**"Other CA streamer":** no other California streamer is a comparably prominent political figure. Minor ones include Colin Hernandez (DSA-aligned AD-51 candidate).

## 3. Democratic Socialists of America (DSA)
**What it says it wants** (national program ["Workers Deserve More," July 2026](https://program.dsausa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/WDM-Program.pdf)):
- Universal no-cost healthcare, a 32-hour week and universal rent control.
- Demilitarize police "as steps towards fully abolishing the police and prison system."
- "Defund the Department of War." End aid to Israel. Abolish ICE.
- End sanctions on Cuba, Venezuela and Iran.
- Abolish the Electoral College and the Senate. "Replace the President and Supreme Court with an executive and judiciary chosen by and subordinate to Congress."
- Public ownership of the largest corporations.

**Officeholders:**
- NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani (since Jan 1, 2026). DSA claimed his win in an [NPC statement](https://www.dsausa.org/statements/zohran-mamdani-wins-national-political-committee-statement/).
- Rep. Rashida Tlaib, called a "Detroit DSA member" in DSA's 2019 article.
- NY state legislators (*reported*).

Bell (KY), Bojak (NY), Nichols (AZ), Hairston (TX), Carpenter (OR), Broderick (VT), Oliver Larkin (US House), Thomason (NV), Floyd (FL), Parr (KY) and Chris Rabb (PA-3). Outcomes (*reported*): Rabb won his primary; Larkin lost. Melat Kiros won CO-1 and Claire Valdez won NY-7, but neither endorsement appears on the national site.

**2026 statements** ([index](https://www.dsausa.org/statements/)):

- May: VRA; Nakba; "Lying About Cuba Again."
