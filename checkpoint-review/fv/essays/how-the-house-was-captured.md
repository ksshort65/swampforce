## How the House was captured

- Slug: how-the-house-was-captured
- Series: The Parties
- Date: 2026-08-29

Not a movie syndicate. A paying club. Both parties kept the books.

A protection racket sells a shield and collects dues. Washington does a legal version. Taxes. Donor access. The employee writes a rule a donor wanted, then walks into the firm that asked for it. That is a revolving door. A career path. Not a filed racketeering case.

This series is the map. Committees that never shrink. Thousand-page bills nobody reads. A “fight” on television and a handshake on the roll call. Both parties did this. If a lesson only names one jersey, it is a clip. We will name the jobs: leadership PACs, lobby shops, trade associations, the offices that write the draft and the firms that hire the drafter.

> A republic is principals and agents. A club is dues and protection.

Lesson two is the money. Lesson three is the door. Not left versus right. The people who stay versus the people who work.
