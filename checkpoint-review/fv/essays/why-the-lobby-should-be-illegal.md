## Why the lobby should be illegal

- Slug: why-the-lobby-should-be-illegal
- Series: Congress
- Date: 2026-08-29

Paid influence is not speech. It is a second government the people did not hire.

Citizens may walk to the Capitol and tell an employee what they think. That is petition. A firm that is paid to live inside the political club, draft the bill, and hire last year’s staffer is not petition. It is a second government. The first is on the ballot. The second is not.

Why it is not banned: the First Amendment covers petition, and the Court has treated money as speech in campaigns. Why it still should be fenced: disclosure is not a fence. The Lobbying Disclosure Act of 1995 is a form. Forms do not stop the door from revolving. Later lessons: FARA (foreign money), the cooling-off period that is not cool, and a simple rule we will argue — if they write the law, they cannot sell it for a set number of years.

> The people hire Congress. The lobby hires Congress back.
