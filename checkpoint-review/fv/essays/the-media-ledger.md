### What repetition does

Psychological warfare, on this page, is not a secret military program and it is not a basement plot. It is a method, and the method is ordinary. A short false sentence is easier to remember than a transcript. If a person hears that sentence every day, for months or for years, the mind stores it as something they saw. They will argue for it. They will not go find the tape, because the caption already feels like the tape.

The correction is long. The lie is six seconds, and it is repeated on the hour. That imbalance is the weapon. After enough repetitions, people defend the caption as if they watched the recording, and they sort a neighbor into an enemy without a statute. The length under each row is the point. That is how long the country was told to treat the caption as a fact.

### Why the slogan

The slogans were not descriptions. They were permission. Fine people. Bloodbath. Dictator. Bleach. Collusion. Threat to democracy. Clear and present danger. Each one was built to move an elected opponent out of politics and into the category of an extinction event. Once a man is an extinction event, the next sentence is always the same: ordinary law is too slow, so you may break it to stop him.

Clear and present danger is the cleanest case. Holmes wrote it in Schenck. [Brandenburg v. Ohio, 395 U.S. 444](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/395/444/) replaced it. The government may punish speech only if it is intended to produce imminent lawless action and is likely to produce it. That sentence limits the state. It was used as the opposite of itself. Repeat the phrase until the public hears a finding. Then the FISA footnotes do not matter. The uncut tape is called context. A parody can stand in for the memo. A docket can be timed to an election and called accountability. [5 U.S.C. § 3331](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/5/3331) is an oath to the Constitution, without any mental reservation. The charter has no clause that lets you break it to save it.

That is why the list below is long. A single lie can be corrected. A slogan, repeated, becomes the reason the next lie is allowed. The correction is not a favor to him. It is the minimum a republic owes the people who were told the law had been suspended.
