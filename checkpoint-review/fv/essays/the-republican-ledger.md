## The Republican ledger

- Slug: the-republican-ledger
- Series: Republicans
- Date: 2026-09-22

What the party promised the country, and what it did. The same standard. The other thumb on the scale.

The same standard cuts this way. A party that sells itself as the correction does not get to clip the tape, skip the court, or promise a check it will not write. This page is the Republican ledger: the promise, the chant, and the statute that did not follow.

Mexico did not pay for the wall. The repeal did not pass. The taxes were not released. The budget was not balanced. The 2020 cases did not prove an outcome-changing theft. January 6 was not a tour. The New York jury did return 34 counts. A party that will not say those sentences is asking for the same trust it says the other side forfeited.

Older betrayals stay because the country still pays them. Iraq was sold as a certainty about weapons that were not in the ground. “Read my lips” was followed by a tax increase. “Mission accomplished” was a banner, not the end of the war. A party does not get a clean start by changing the subject.

> A correction that only runs one direction is not a correction.

### Chart rows

**Mexico pays**

They ran: Mexico will pay for the wall.

The file: Mexico did not pay. Congress appropriated American money. A chant is not a wire transfer.

**Repeal**

They ran: Repeal and replace on day one.

The file: Full Republican control in 2017. The repeal failed on the Senate floor. The promise was the campaign.

**Lock her up**

They ran: Lock her up.

The file: No Clinton prosecution followed. The chant was a feeling. A charge sheet is a document.

**The returns**

They ran: I will release my tax returns.

The file: He did not release them. That broken promise is his. The 2020 publication was a theft. Charles Littlejohn pleaded guilty under 26 U.S.C. § 7213 and was sentenced to five years. The forms Ways and Means later released show income tax paid in 2015 through 2019, and $0 in 2020 on a reported loss. The leak is not a finding that tax due went unpaid.

Record: https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-irs-contractor-sentenced-disclosing-tax-return-information-news-organizations

**Balance it**

They ran: We are the party of the balance sheet.

The file: The debt rose by trillions in the first term. A party that will not pass twelve appropriations bills does not get the slogan.

Record: https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/datasets/debt-to-the-penny/debt-to-the-penny

**Proven stolen**

They ran: The 2020 election was proven stolen in court.

The file: Dozens of cases were filed. The outcome-changing proof was not established. A loss in court is not a win you forgot to mention.

**2,000 Mules**

They ran: The film proved the ballots.

The file: A movie is not a verdict. State investigators did not adopt it as the reason the election flipped.

**A tour**

They ran: January 6 was a normal tour.

The file: Officers were assaulted. The building was breached. People died. Calling it a tour is the same clipping, from the other thumb.

**No one was armed**

They ran: No one was armed. It was a protest.

The file: Weapons cases were charged. Most defendants were not charged with firearms inside the chamber. “None” is the lie. “All of them” is the other lie.

**Antifa did it**

They ran: Antifa staged January 6.

The file: The FBI’s case list is not an Antifa list. A theory that moves the blame off the people on the tape is a caption.

**Pence can flip it**

They ran: The vice president can reject the electoral votes.

The file: The Twelfth Amendment makes him the presiding officer, not a second electorate. The theory was rejected. The pressure on him was real.

**Find 11,780**

They ran: It was only about legitimate ballots.

The file: The Georgia call is on tape. “I just want to find 11,780 votes” is in it. The sentence stays. It is not sanded.

**Perfect call**

They ran: A perfect call. Nothing to see.

The file: The memo contains the ask about the Bidens. Looking at corruption can be the job. Calling the memo empty is not.

**Fight like hell**

They ran: He only said peacefully.

The file: He said both. “Peacefully and patriotically” and “fight like hell” are on one Ellipse tape. Owning only the soft line is clipping.

**We read the bills**

They ran: We are the adults who read the bill.

The file: Continuing resolutions and thousand-page omnibus bills passed with hours to read. Both parties. The claim of innocence is the lie.

**Term limits**

They ran: We support term limits.

The file: The members who say it keep the seat. A pledge that never reaches a vote is a brochure.

**The 34 counts**

They ran: The New York case is fake. Ignore the verdict.

The file: A jury convicted on 34 counts of falsifying business records. The novel predicate is why it is on appeal. “Fake” erases the jury. That is not the file.

**I alone**

They ran: I alone can fix it.

The file: Article I still holds the purse. One man does not appropriate. The sentence was a campaign. The charter did not move.

**Infrastructure week**

They ran: Infrastructure week. It is coming.

The file: The week was announced and announced. The major bill did not pass in that term.

**No new taxes**

They ran: Read my lips. No new taxes.

The file: George H. W. Bush, 1988. The 1990 budget deal raised them. The mouth and the statute did not match.

**The weapons**

They ran: The weapons of mass destruction are there. It is a certainty.

The file: The Iraq Survey Group did not find the stockpiles the country was promised. A war sold on a certainty that was not in the ground is a betrayal.

**Mission accomplished**

They ran: Major combat operations in Iraq have ended.

The file: May 1, 2003. The war had not ended. The banner was early.

**Death panels**

They ran: The health bill creates death panels.

The file: The phrase was a caption for end-of-life counseling language. It was not a board that voted on who dies.

**Deficits don’t matter**

They ran: Reagan proved deficits don’t matter.

The file: The debt is the matter. The grandchildren pay the interest. A shrug is not a budget.

**We back the blue**

They ran: We back the blue.

The file: Then a faction called the beaten officers actors, and called the breach a tour. You do not back someone you will not name.

**We have his back**

They ran: The party defends him. The record is in good hands.

The file: The caption was that he is the most corrupt president in history. It ran every week. The answer was short enough to say on the floor: the tax forms were stolen, 26 U.S.C. § 7213, and the man who stole them got five years; the income-tax bills are on the Ways and Means release; the 34 counts are false records, not bribery. The comparison is the Biden bank records, the Burisma seat, and a pardon. The House opened an inquiry and did not make the country sit with that file. A party that will not read its own exhibits out loud is not a defense.
