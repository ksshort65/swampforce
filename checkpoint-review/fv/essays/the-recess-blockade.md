## The recess blockade

- Slug: the-recess-blockade
- Series: Congress
- Date: 2026-08-23

Pro forma gavels. Fake sessions. A president who cannot appoint. Article II, gutted on purpose.

Clinton: 139 recess appointments. Bush: 171. Obama: 32 until the Court stripped the tactic. Trump first term: 0. Biden: 0 — the Senate was aligned. Trump now: 0, because someone still walks into an empty chamber every few days, bangs a gavel, and leaves.

[NLRB v. Noel Canning (2014)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/573/12-1281/) said a valid recess lasts at least ten consecutive days, and the Senate decides when it is in session. Armed with that, a faction invented fake sessions — no business, one politician, a few seconds. Fiction with a gavel. It turns off [Article II, Section 2, Clause 3](https://constitution.congress.gov/constitution/article-2/) on purpose so a president the country just elected cannot staff the government.

The Framers wrote the Recess Appointments Clause as a check against a Senate that would not act. Fake sessions are a check against the voters. That is the 2024 mandate dying in an empty room. Track the calendar. Count the days between gavels. The numbers are the argument.
