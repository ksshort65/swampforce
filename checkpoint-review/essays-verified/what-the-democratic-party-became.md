## What the Democrats became

- Slug: what-the-democratic-party-became
- Series: The Parties
- Date: 2026-08-29

**Our view:** A party of farmers and unions became a party of campuses, agencies, and clips.

**Our view:** This is not a hate page. It is a history class. Jefferson’s party talked limited government. Jackson’s party talked the common man and still built a spoils machine. The 20th century added unions, the New Deal, and Jim Crow in the same tent. The 1960s split it: [civil-rights votes](https://www.congress.gov/bill/88th-congress/house-bill/7152) that were right, and a new class of staffers who never left campus.

**Our view:** By the 2020s the brand was agencies, identity memos, and a newsroom that treated the party as weather. Working counties left. The clip stayed. Later lessons: the machines, the 1964–68 break, the donor shift, and what the 2024 loss actually was. We will give them the wins too — Social Security’s passage, the 1964 Act — or this is a caption with our flag on it.

> **Our view:** A party is a tool. When the tool stops serving the people who built it, name the date.
