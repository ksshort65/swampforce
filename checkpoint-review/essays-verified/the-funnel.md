## The funnel

- Slug: the-funnel
- Series: Congress
- Date: 2026-09-21

**Our view:** Taxpayer to agency to NGO. USAID. FEMA. The Endowment. There is no line item that says DNC. There is a pipe. Congress votes the water.

Image: /images/chart-funnel.jpg — Three pipes: USAID, FEMA NGOs, NED

The money does not leave the Treasury as a check to a party. It leaves as an appropriation. Congress votes it. An agency writes a grant. A nongovernmental organization cashes it. That is the funnel. [USASpending](https://www.usaspending.gov/agency/agency-for-international-development) is the ledger. [ForeignAssistance.gov](https://www.foreignassistance.gov/) is the map. In fiscal 2023 the United States disbursed about **$80.5 billion** in foreign assistance, by ForeignAssistance.gov’s current data. The implementers are contractors and NGOs. They are not the DNC and they are not the RNC. They are paid with the same taxes. The political work happens after the grant hits the letterhead.

[USAID’s Inspector General](https://oig.usaid.gov/) is the file on what happens inside the pipe. [Cash assistance in the West Bank and Gaza](https://oig.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2026-05/Audit%20of%20USAID%20Cash%20Assistance%20to%20West%20Bank%20Gaza%20%20%289-000-26-002-P%29.pdf): about **$36 million** in fiscal 2024 to four NGOs, with key fraud risks USAID did not identify. [Humanitarian awards in that theater](https://oig.usaid.gov/sites/default/files/2026-05/Final%20Audit%20Report%20-%20West%20Bank%20and%20Gaza%20Partner%20Vetting%20Audit%20%288-294-26-003-P%29_0.pdf): **$650 million** across eighteen awards, with selective partner vetting that the auditor said could increase the risk of diversion. That is not a panel. That is the auditor. At home the same shape: [DHS OIG-26-04](https://www.oig.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/assets/2026-04/OIG-26-04-Apr26.pdf) — FEMA awarded nearly **$1.4 billion** in fiscal 2023–24 through the Emergency Food and Shelter Humanitarian program and the Shelter and Services Program to states, cities, and nonprofits. The Inspector General could not ensure the money was used as the law required. CBP transferred the pile. NGOs and local governments cashed it. [Who got paid](/dispatch/who-got-paid) is the border half of the same pipe.

**Our view:** Federal grant money is not supposed to buy a campaign. [2 CFR 200.450](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-2/section-200.450) forbids using the award to lobby. That is the rule. The ecosystem is the rest. [22 U.S.C. § 4411](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/22/4411) created the National Endowment for Democracy. Congress funds it. Fiscal 2024 kept it at **$315 million**. The Endowment grants to institutes the parties already know: the National Democratic Institute and the International Republican Institute. NED funds four core institutes, including those two party-affiliated ones. It is not a wire to a national committee. Say the difference. Keep the pipe. [The FEC](https://www.fec.gov/introduction-campaign-finance/understanding-ways-support-federal-candidates/presidential-elections/public-funding-presidential-elections/) still offers a **$3** checkoff on the 1040 for the Presidential Election Campaign Fund. That box is voluntary. It does not raise the tax. Public funding of nominating conventions ended in **2014**. Recent major-party nominees have not taken the general-election grant. Anyone who says “USAID funds the Democratic Party” is running a caption. Anyone who says the Treasury stops at the NGO door is running the other one. Congress votes the appropriation. The agency writes the grant. The NGO spends it. The parties live in the weather that money makes.

> **Our view:** There is no line item that says DNC. There is a funnel. Congress opens it.
