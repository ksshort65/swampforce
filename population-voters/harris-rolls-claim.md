# Claim check: Kamala Harris on "purging" voter rolls (about Sep 22–23, 2026)

**Claim as the user heard it:** Harris said on TV "yesterday" (about Sep 23, 2026) that people should check their voter registration because Trump is purging the voter rolls.

**SwampForce label (factual claim only): Rated misleading.** This applies to the claim as the user phrased it, "Trump is purging the voter rolls." Her advice to check registration status is not a factual claim, so it is not rated.

## 2. What the federal government has actually done on voter rolls (2025–2026)

| Action | What it does | Status per court and official records |
|---|---|---|
| **EO 14248** (Mar 25, 2025; 90 FR 14005) | §2(a): proof of citizenship on the federal form. §2(b): DHS gives states free SAVE access and, with DOGE, reviews state voter lists against immigration databases. §2(c): shares information on registered or voting foreign nationals with the AG and states. §3(a): SSA data offered to states. §3(c): AG action against states on NVRA/HAVA list maintenance. **No section directs a federal agency to remove anyone from a roll.** | *LULAC v. EOP* (D.D.C. 25-cv-946), Oct 31, 2025: §2(a) permanently enjoined. Jan 30, 2026: §§2(d) and 3(d) held unconstitutional and enjoined; SAVE/Numident Privacy Act injunction request denied as moot, but the court declared DHS/SSA must strictly follow the Privacy Act. Other parts deferred; appeals pending. |
| **EO 14399** (Mar 2026; 91 FR 17125) | DHS to create "State Citizenship Lists" and send them to states; states are not required to use them. | The D. Mass. injunction was stayed by SCOTUS in *Trump v. California*, No. 26A124 (Aug 24, 2026), on standing. Legality was not decided. |
| **DOJ demands and suits for full state voter lists** | Requests began May 2025. DOJ has sued 30 states plus DC to obtain unredacted lists. | Per NCSL (Sep 15, 2026) and the UW tracker, 22+ suits have been dismissed, including Colorado on Aug 4, 2026 and Michigan (affirmed by the 6th Circuit Aug 14, 2026). Oklahoma settled. Several are pending. 13 states provided full lists. **A list transfer is not a removal.** |
| **DHS/USCIS SAVE bulk voter checks** | Since May 2025, "more than 65 million voters across 26 States" were verified as citizens and **28,635 were flagged as potential non-citizens** (government's own figure, SCOTUS application No. 26A308). | *LWV v. DHS* (D.D.C.), Jun 22, 2026: the SAVE "modified system" and its Privacy Act notices were vacated. Stays were denied by the district court (Jul 8) and the D.C. Circuit (Sep 4). The SCOTUS stay application is pending. DHS told SCOTUS that a SAVE response "changes no voter's status; only the State's independent decision to question or cancel a registration does that." |
| **USCIS "Unlawful Voter Initiative"** (whistleblower allegations, Sep 14, 2026) | Officers allegedly told to search state voter lookup sites and create federal records; quotas alleged. | These are allegations only. The allegations do not say DHS removed anyone from a roll. |
| **DOJ prosecutions** | 70 people charged with unlawful voting or registration offenses, Jan 2025–Sep 2026. HSI reports ~1,600 cases and 160 arrests (AP, Sep 23, 2026). DOJ charged 16 more on Sep 18, 2026. | Prosecutions of individuals, not roll removals. |

**Who removes voters.** Voter rolls are kept by **states and counties** under NVRA §8 (52 U.S.C. 20507) and HAVA. I found **no record of any federal entity removing anyone from a state voter roll.**

**State removals driven by federal data**
- **Texas** (SOS, Sep 15, 2026). The state ran 18M registrants through SAVE and referred 2,724 potential noncitizens to counties. 578 of them later showed they were citizens (506 because passport data was added to SAVE after the run). Counties were told to reinstate any of the 578 they had removed. 117 cases went to the AG.
  - The total number actually cancelled was **not reported**. The number of citizens removed before reinstatement is **not reported**.
- **Louisiana** (Sep 2025). 390 suspected noncitizens identified via SAVE out of about 2.9M registrants; 79 had voted at some point since the 1980s.
- **Idaho and Utah.** Small counts reported (36 and 27). These come from secondary reports and were not verified from primary records.
- **Scale.** For comparison, states remove millions of registrations every cycle under ordinary list maintenance. EAVS shows 21,298,175 removals in 2022–24, mostly for moving, death, and failure to respond to NVRA notices.

## 3. Rating and reasoning

- **Claim rated:** "Trump is purging the voter rolls" (user's version). Harris's literal words were "They are cheating by purging voter rolls."
- **Label: Rated misleading.**

**What supports part of the claim**
- The administration has pushed hard for removals of noncitizens: two executive orders, 30+ DOJ suits for voter lists, mass SAVE checks, and a USCIS review effort.
- That push has led some states to question and cancel registrations. In Texas, it swept in hundreds of people later shown to be citizens.

**What the claim leaves out or gets wrong**
- No federal entity removes voters, and the government itself says SAVE results change no one's status.
- Most of the federal measures have been blocked, vacated, or dismissed in court.
- The confirmed state removals tied to federal data are in the hundreds or low thousands. The only documented citizen flags (Texas, 578) came with instructions to reinstate.
- The record I reviewed does not show a federal "purge" of eligible voters.
- "Cheating" is a characterization, not a checkable fact.
- The claim that Section 5 of the VRA "is no longer available" refers to Supreme Court rulings, not executive action. It is outside this check and not rated.

**Recommended wording.** "The administration has pushed states to check voter rolls for noncitizens using federal data, and some states have cancelled registrations as a result (including some citizens later reinstated in Texas); no federal agency has removed voters itself, and most of the federal measures are tied up or blocked in court."
