## Claim-by-claim

| # | Claim in letter | Label | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The bill "would require states to obtain proof of U.S. citizenship when individuals register to vote in a federal election." | **Accurate** | Both texts amend NVRA §4(b): "the State shall not accept and process an application to register to vote in an election for Federal office unless the applicant presents documentary proof of United States citizenship." New §8(j)(1) says the same. It applies to applications submitted after enactment; it does not re-verify people already registered. |
| 2 | "It would also require states to remove non-citizens from existing voter rolls." | **Accurate** | Both texts add NVRA §8(k): "A State shall remove an individual who is not a citizen... at any time upon receipt of documentation or verified information." They also require states to take ongoing "affirmative steps" (§8(j)(3)–(4)). The SAVE America Act goes further: states must submit their **complete** voter list to DHS for SAVE comparison and remove noncitizens after notice and a chance to prove citizenship (§8(j)(4)(B)). |
| 3 | "state-issued driver's licenses wouldn't be sufficient for eligible voters to prove their citizenship." | **Accurate** (for registration) | The accepted-document list includes "(1) A form of identification issued consistent with the requirements of the REAL ID Act of 2005 **that indicates the applicant is a citizen**." A standard REAL ID license, including Colorado's, does not show citizenship; Enhanced Driver's Licenses in a few other states do. Para (4) accepts a government photo ID "showing that the applicant's place of birth was in the United States," which ordinary licenses don't show. A license counts only **together with** a birth certificate, naturalization certificate or similar record (para 5). Context: SAVE America Act §3 (photo ID to *vote*) does accept a state driver's license at the polls. The letter's statement concerns proving citizenship, and is accurate for that. |
| 4 | "the bill would not give states any additional funding to implement these new restrictions." | **Accurate** | Neither text contains an appropriation or an authorization of appropriations; I searched both engrossed texts. The only money-related provision bars federal agencies from charging states fees for verification responses (§8(j)(5)(E)). |
| 5 | "noncitizens voting in federal elections is already illegal and punishable under existing law." | **Accurate** | See the statute list below this table. |
| 6 | "It's also incredibly rare for a noncitizen to even attempt to vote in U.S. elections." | **Accurate** | Official counts are small compared with 211M+ active registrants. See the evidence list below this table. |
| 7 | "even if they did attempt to, we have protections in place to prevent them." | **Accurate** (with limits noted) | See the Colorado safeguards list below this table. |

**Statutes behind claim 5**
- 18 U.S.C. 611: noncitizen voting in a federal election; up to 1 year and a fine.
- 18 U.S.C. 1015(f): false claim of citizenship to register or vote; up to 5 years.
- 18 U.S.C. 911: false claim of citizenship.
- 52 U.S.C. 20511: NVRA criminal penalties for fraudulent registration or voting.
- Immigration consequences: inadmissibility and deportability under INA 212(a)(10)(D) and 237(a)(6).

**Evidence behind claim 6**
- Georgia's 2024 audit found 20 noncitizens among about 8.2M registrants.
- DHS's own SAVE runs flagged 28,635 *potential* noncitizens while verifying 65M+ voters. Flags are not confirmations: in Texas, 578 of 2,724 flagged were later shown to be citizens.
- DOJ has charged 70 people since January 2025.
- Caveats:
  - "Attempts" cannot be measured directly.
  - Some noncitizen registrations come from government errors rather than attempts by the person. New Jersey's MVC glitch registered about 6,600 people who said they were not citizens; about 340 of them voted. Reuters counts more than 30,000 error-driven registrations across 12 states since 2000.

**Colorado safeguards behind claim 7**
- Applicants attest to citizenship under penalty of perjury.
- HAVA (52 U.S.C. 21083(a)(5)) requires matching a driver's license number or the last 4 SSN digits.
- Licenses issued to noncitizens under SB13-251 are flagged in Department of Revenue records and marked not valid for federal ID or voting. Registration attempts with them are rejected (Weld County Clerk, KUNC, Oct 31, 2024).
- The state uses SAVE to check former noncitizen-license holders.
- The Secretary of State and county clerks refer suspect cases to district attorneys. Example: the 2013 referral of 155 people, of whom 4 were charged in Arapahoe County.
- **Limits:**
  - Safeguards have failed elsewhere through DMV errors (New Jersey, California 2018, Oregon, Iowa).
  - Colorado's own 2022 ERIC mailer error sent registration *postcards* (not forms or ballots) to about 30,000 noncitizens. The Secretary of State said none were known to have registered.

## Colorado registration context
- **AVR and the DMV.** Colorado registers eligible people automatically at the DMV. In 2024 there were 2,202,812 AVR transactions and 0 non-AVR motor-vehicle transactions (EAVS 2024).
- **Noncitizen licenses.** People holding SB13-251 noncitizen licenses are excluded because DMV citizenship-status data flags them. The exact statutory citation was not verified in this pass.
- **Noncitizens in Colorado.** ACS 2024 estimates 332,000 noncitizen residents. SSA reports 2,806 noncitizen SSI recipients in Colorado (Dec 2024).
