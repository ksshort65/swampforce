# Government-wide fraud and improper payments: running tally

*Last compiled Sep 24, 2026 (MT). Every figure below comes from a primary government source: paymentaccuracy.gov/OMB data, GAO, CIGIE/oversight.gov, DOJ, or SBA OIG. The data file is `fraud-tally.csv`.*

## Read this first: why there is no single "grand total of fraud"

The five categories below measure **different things**, and they **overlap**. Adding them together would double count.

| Category | What it measures | Is it fraud? |
|---|---|---|
| Improper payments (OMB/agency estimates) | Payments that should not have been made, were the wrong amount, or could not be documented ("unknown") | **Not all fraud.** This includes underpayments, paperwork and documentation errors, and eligibility errors. Agencies do not break out the fraud share. |
| GAO fraud estimate | A statistical estimate of direct annual losses to fraud | Estimated fraud. It partly overlaps improper payments and excludes revenue (tax) fraud. |
| IG questioned costs / funds put to better use | Audit recommendations | Not losses. These amounts may never be disallowed or realized. |
| IG investigative recoveries / DOJ False Claims Act | Money ordered or recovered through enforcement | Documented enforcement outcomes. They overlap each other, because OIG investigations feed DOJ cases. |
| DOGE savings | Claimed cancellations | Not a fraud measure. GAO found the claims largely unverifiable (see below). |

**Method used here:** the tally keeps separate running totals by tier and never sums across tiers.

- **Tier 1 (measured error, agency-estimated):** improper payments, about **$3 trillion cumulative from FY2003 to FY2025** (GAO). The OMB dataset sum for FY2004–FY2025 is $2,984.0B. The FY2025 figure is **$186B**.
- **Tier 2 (estimated fraud):** GAO puts fraud at **$233B–$521B per year** (based on FY2018–2022 data). Estimated pandemic fraud: SBA OIG found more than $200B potentially fraudulent in PPP and EIDL, and GAO estimates $100B–$135B in UI. Those two cover different programs, so the combined pandemic figure is **about $300B–$335B, estimated or potential, not adjudicated**.
- **Tier 3 (enforcement outcomes):** FY2025 DOJ False Claims Act recoveries were **$6.8B**. CIGIE reported FY2025 investigative receivables and recoveries of **$20.6B**. These two overlap, so they are not added.
- **Tier 4 (claims):** DOGE claimed $215B. **GAO could not verify most of it.**

If a single sentence is needed: *"Federal agencies estimated $186 billion in improper payments in FY2025, about $3 trillion since FY2003; not all of this is fraud. GAO separately estimates fraud costs $233–$521 billion a year."*

## 2. GAO's government-wide fraud estimate
GAO-24-105833 (Apr 2024) estimates that the federal government loses **$233 billion to $521 billion a year** to fraud, based on FY2018–FY2022 data. The estimate excludes revenue fraud. It is a statistical estimate, not a count of proven cases, and it overlaps improper payments. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-105833

## 4. DOJ False Claims Act recoveries
FY2025: **more than $6.8B** in settlements and judgments, a record. Of that, $5.7B related to health care and $5.3B came from qui tam (whistleblower) cases. 1,297 qui tam suits were filed. Total recoveries since 1986 exceed $85B. https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/false-claims-act-settlements-and-judgments-exceed-68b-fiscal-year-2025
Statute: 31 U.S.C. 3729–3733. https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title31-section3729&num=0&edition=prelim

## 5. Pandemic program fraud
- **SBA OIG Report 23-09 (June 27, 2023):** more than **$200B potentially fraudulent**, made up of EIDL $136B and PPP $64B. That is about 17% of roughly $1.2T disbursed. About $30B in EIDL funds was seized or returned. "Potentially fraudulent" is the OIG's term and is not an adjudicated amount. https://www.oversight.gov/sites/default/files/documents/reports/2023-06/SBA-OIG-Report-23-09.pdf
- **GAO-23-106696 (Sept 2023):** estimated UI fraud during the pandemic was **likely $100B–$135B**. States themselves reported finding $5.3B in fraudulent overpayments and recovering $1.2B of it. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106696
- **GAO-23-105523:** $878B in UI benefits was paid from Apr 2020 to Sep 2022. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-105523
- **GAO-23-106556:** at least **1,044 people convicted** of pandemic program fraud as of Jan 2023. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-23-106556
- Pandemic Response Accountability Committee portal: https://www.pandemicoversight.gov/

## 6. DOGE savings claims versus the GAO audit
GAO-26-108615 (Aug 6, 2026), https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108615/index.html:
- DOGE's website claimed about **$215B** in savings (unaudited). Its itemized "Wall of Receipts" totaled **$110.34B**: contracts $61.02B, grants $49.21B, leases $113M.
- **Contracts:** only 27.5% ($16.8B) of claimed contract savings followed DOGE's own stated method. $27.4B was claimed on contracts where no termination action was taken. Examples: a DHA contract claimed at $1.7B saved had no action taken, and an Air Force BIM contract was claimed at $4B against a $3.75B value reduction.
- **Grants:** GAO could not verify 96% ($47.32B) of claimed grant savings.
- **Leases:** DOGE claimed $113M, but its own entries sum to $53.5M, and GAO calculated $31.8M. That is an $81.1M overstatement. 108 of the leases were already being terminated before DOGE existed.
- The DOGE temporary organization ended July 4, 2026. Its website was last updated Jan 1, 2026.
- **Verdict on the headline DOGE savings figure: Disproven as stated.** GAO found that the itemized claims were not supported at the amounts claimed. Some real savings exist (at least $16.8B in contract savings computed on DOGE's method), but no audited total is available.
- For context, a political reaction to the report from Senate HSGAC Democrats (a lead, not a finding): https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/media/dems/peters-blumenthal-release-gao-report-finding-doge-misled-americans-about-claimed-savings/

## Claims the record does not support
- "Improper payments = fraud": **Disproven.** OMB and GAO define improper payments to include underpayments and documentation errors.
- Adding improper payments, the GAO fraud estimate and IG and DOJ recoveries into one "waste and fraud" number is double counting. **Do not do this.**
- DOGE's "$215B saved" / "$110B receipts": **not supported by GAO's audit** at the claimed amounts.
