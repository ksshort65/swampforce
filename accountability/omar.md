# Rep. Ilhan Omar: the 2009 marriage / immigration-fraud allegation

*Research file for swampforce.com. Compiled Sep 24, 2026 (MT). Primary sources first; anything found only in news reporting is labeled **reported, not confirmed by primary record**.*

## The claim
Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-Minn.) married Ahmed Nur Said Elmi in 2009, Elmi is her brother, and the marriage was a sham meant to get him an immigration benefit.

## Verdict: **Unresolved (no official finding)**

No court, prosecutor, agency, or congressional ethics body has published a finding that Elmi is Omar's brother or that the marriage was fraudulent. No charge, indictment, civil denaturalization complaint, or removal case has been filed. In 2026, senior officials said an investigation is open: VP Vance said DOJ is looking at it (May 19), and border czar Homan said HSI/ICE is investigating (Sept. 13). DHS Secretary Mullin has said publicly "we know" she married her brother, but he has produced no document supporting that, and DHS says it does not comment on ongoing investigations. An open investigation is not a finding. Until a charging document, court ruling, or official report is published, the claim cannot be labeled Proven. It also cannot be labeled Disproven, because no official body has cleared it either. Public records have not established the relationship one way or the other.

**Do not publish** the claim as fact ("married her brother") or as a crime. Some related facts are documented, and those can be published (see below).
