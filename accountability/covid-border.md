# COVID-19 and the border: orders, encounters, Title 42, origin assessments, claim checks

*Compiled Sep 24, 2026 (MT). This page uses primary records only: the Federal Register, state executive orders, CBP data, court opinions, ODNI, and congressional reports. Reporting appears only as a lead and is labeled when used. The data file is `covid-border.csv`, where the `record_type` column separates the timeline, monthly encounters, origin assessments and claim checks.*

| Date | Body | Finding (their words or close paraphrase) | Type | Source |

| Jun 18, 2026 | DNI Gabbard | Released declassified documents under the heading "Fauci Funded Wuhan Lab Research That Sparked COVID" | Official statement plus documents (listed on the ODNI newsroom) | https://www.dni.gov/index.php/newsroom/press-releases/press-releases-2026 |

Confidence levels (FBI moderate, DOE low) come from the 2021 ODNI document, which does not name the agencies, and from reporting. Director Wray said in a Feb 28, 2023 interview, in his own words, that the FBI assessed a "potential lab incident" as most likely (reported).

**Verdict on "COVID came from a lab leak": Unresolved (no official finding).** U.S. intelligence agencies remain split. FBI, DOE and CIA lean toward a lab origin with low to moderate confidence; the NIC and four agencies lean toward natural origin. No agency claims high confidence. The House majority and the current administration say a lab origin is likely, the House minority and WHO SAGO disagree or call the question open, and no court or adjudicative finding exists. Evidence from the June 2026 ODNI release was reported by independent reviewers (Lawfare, CNN, June 2026, leads only) not to establish causation. **The one point of broad official agreement: the virus was not developed as a biological weapon** (ODNI 2021 and 2023).
