- **Deception form:** `premature "proven" framing`
- **Tag:** `retracted/corrected`
- **Truth source:** https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/brian-sicknick-fire-extinguisher/

- **Notes:** Sources were anonymous LE officials; ME ruled natural causes (stroke). Assault of officers including chemical spray still documented separately.
