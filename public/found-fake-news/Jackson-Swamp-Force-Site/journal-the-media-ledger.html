The verified text, checked against primary records on Sep 24, 2026. Passages marked Our view are opinion.

Our view The chart of captions sold as fact. Epstein is on it. The crimes were real. The client list, the murder finding, and the one-party scandal were not in the file.

This file holds 93 captions. Some are a cut tape or the wrong picture. Most are a word that was added, widened, or swapped until the sentence became a crime, a finding, or an order the recording does not contain. A few are the unequal standard in plain sight: one party’s scandal cut from logs that show both, one set of children used as a slogan while another count of children was treated as politics, a faith named when the order named countries, and one candidate called a danger so the law could be broken to stop him. A network is not a trustee under the United States Code. It asked the country to trust it with the record. An accurate report was the job it sold. These 93 lines are the breach of that job. The nation was divided along the caption. These 93 are the captions in this file. They are not every line that aired in twelve years.

What repetition does

Our view Psychological warfare, on this page, is not a secret military program and it is not a basement plot. It is a method, and the method is ordinary. A short false sentence is easier to remember than a transcript. If a person hears that sentence every day, for months or for years, the mind stores it as something they saw. They will argue for it. They will not go find the tape, because the caption already feels like the tape.

Our view The correction is long. The lie is six seconds, and it is repeated on the hour. That imbalance is the weapon. After enough repetitions, people defend the caption as if they watched the recording, and they sort a neighbor into an enemy without a statute. The length under each row is the point. That is how long the country was told to treat the caption as a fact.

Epstein

Epstein is on this chart because the crimes are real and the outrage was built past the file. He pleaded guilty in Florida in 2008. He was indicted again in New York in 2019. Ghislaine Maxwell was convicted and sentenced to twenty years. Those sentences are not a caption. What was manufactured is the rest. A client list the Justice Department and the FBI said the investigation did not find. A murder finding the medical examiner did not make. A one-party scandal cut from logs that show both worlds. The department’s July 2025 memorandum said there was no incriminating client list. Our view The victims were then used as a slogan, while the children the government could not account for at the border were treated as a different argument. Both counts are children.

Why the slogan

Our view The slogans were not descriptions. They were permission. Fine people. Bloodbath. Dictator. Bleach. Collusion. Threat to democracy. Clear and present danger. Each one was built to move an elected opponent out of politics and into the category of an extinction event. Once a man is an extinction event, the next sentence is always the same: ordinary law is too slow, so you may break it to stop him.

Clear and present danger is the cleanest case. Holmes wrote it in Schenck. Brandenburg v. Ohio, 395 U.S. 444 replaced it. The government may punish speech only if it is intended to produce imminent lawless action and is likely to produce it. That sentence limits the state. It was used as the opposite of itself. Repeat the phrase until the public hears a finding. Then the FISA footnotes do not matter. The uncut tape is called context. A parody can stand in for the memo. A docket can be timed to an election and called accountability. 5 U.S.C. § 3331 is an oath to the Constitution, without any mental reservation. The charter has no clause that lets you break it to save it.

Our view That is why the list below is long. A single lie can be corrected. A slogan, repeated, becomes the reason the next lie is allowed. The correction is not a favor to him. It is the minimum a republic owes the people who were told the law had been suspended.

Where the hours are

The Speaker’s release is not a speech. In November 2023 Speaker Johnson said the House would post 40,000 of about 44,000 hours and hold about 5 percent back for security. The Committee on House Administration’s Subcommittee on Oversight put the public Capitol Police CCTV on Rumble. The committee’s own notice of the releases is here. A public viewing room in the Capitol was what they offered while the website was still a fraction of the archive. The room was in Washington. The country was not. Calling a trip to the Capitol “public access” left the hours inaccessible to anyone who could not get there. On November 17, 2023, Speaker Johnson said the House would start posting video and that the room would let a citizen watch every minute uncensored in the meantime. He also said about 5 percent would stay back for security, and the online tapes were going to have faces blurred. The first post that day was about 90 hours, not 44,000. By March 22, 2024, the committee said almost 13,000 hours were online. On August 19, 2024, it posted another 6,000. A promise to release the tapes, followed by a room the country cannot travel to and a website that is still catching up, is not a release. The Epoch Times made two films, and a film is not the archive either. The Real Story of January 6 premiered July 22, 2022. The Real Story of January 6, Part 2: The Long Road Home followed in January 2024.

Said. Not invented.

These are not in the lie column, because he said them. “Fight like hell.” “Stand back and stand by.” “When the looting starts, the shooting starts.” The Access Hollywood tape, which he called “locker room talk. I’m not proud of it.” “I just want to find 11,780 votes.” The December 2022 post: a fraud that size “allows for the termination of all rules, regulations, and articles, even those found in the Constitution.” “Poisoning the blood.” “Vermin.” He told Bob Woodward on tape, March 19, 2020, “I wanted to always play it down.” A journal that hides those is doing the same work as the package that hides “peacefully and patriotically.”

Our view The caption is not the recording. Read across.
