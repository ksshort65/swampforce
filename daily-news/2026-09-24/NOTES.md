## Facts coverage often leaves out (documented)
1. **The TRO is due-process only.** It does not hold that the ban violates the First Amendment (ECF 24 p.2; Volokh). Outlets that said this correctly: MS NOW, TIME, Volokh.
2. **The President is carved out of the order** (ECF 24 ¶2). TIME and ABC Australia said the judge ordered Trump (candidates 01 and 02).
3. **The judge is a Trump appointee who ruled the same way in 2018** (Acosta). Widely reported.
4. **The White House's compliance timeline** (ECF 27/28) is left out of stories framed as "defies" (watchlist W-04).
5. **The White House offered a post-revocation response process** (letters with a Sep 25 deadline). The judge found it came after the fact and was too vague, but it exists. Some coverage says only "no notice or process."
6. **The judge did not make a final national-security finding.** He said the record was lacking "at least on this record" and reserved the question for a fuller record.
7. **2025 AP dispute ≠ total ban.** The AP kept its hard passes (AP v. Budowich, ECF 46 p.9). See candidate 03.

## Method and dedupe
- Primary record: the full CourtListener docket (ECF 1–28; ECF 27/28 are scanned images I read directly), the White House letters (ECF 16-1/2/3), Roll Call Factba.se and C-SPAN caption transcripts of the Sep 18 Oval Office remarks, and the White House video page.
- Dedupe: checked all 250 rows of `/workspace/term-split/*.csv` and the 7 rows of `/workspace/checkpoint-review/catalog-candidates.csv` for dictator/authoritarian/press pass/hard pass/credential/Acosta/AP/BBC/Pentagon/pool terms. None of the 3 candidates duplicates an existing case. Catalog files were not modified.
- **Checked and rejected (not candidates):**
  - WHCA and NBC saying Trump called it a "ban on the free press": the words are his (W-01).
  - Guardian on the BBC: Trump did previously claim the BBC used AI, so this was downgraded to W-08.
  - Guardian: Trump gave an "inaccurate account" of the ABC/CBS settlements. ABC's $16M total is roughly right, but his CBS "$38 million" figure differs from the reported $16M settlement, so "inaccurate" is defensible.
  - CNN (Stelter), Sep 18: "no indication his administration had taken steps." True at publication time.
  - NYT/Inquirer: Trump "provided no details." True on Sep 19; the letters came Sep 22.
  - Variety calling Kelly a judge "of the D.C. Circuit": an error, but not unfavorable to Trump.
- Headlines that were changed after publication (TheWrap's "Judge Says Trump Must Reverse Press Ban"; Axios's "White House defies court order…") show only in search indexes. The live pages now carry different headlines, so they are not used as candidates.
- Nothing in the fetched content tried to give instructions.
