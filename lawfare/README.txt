PER-CASE STATUS SUMMARY (September 24, 2026)
---------------------------------
- People of the State of New York v. Donald J. Trump et al.: Liability under Exec. Law § 63(12) affirmed by Appellate Division (fractured); entire disgorgement award vacated as an excessive fine (Eighth Amendment); counsel sanctions vacated; Court of Appeals review pending.
- People of the State of New York v. Donald J. Trump: Guilty on 34 felony counts (May 30, 2024); unconditional discharge (Jan. 10, 2025); appeal and federal removal efforts pending; no incarceration.
- United States v. Donald J. Trump, Waltine Nauta, and Carlos De Oliveira: Indictment dismissed (Appointments Clause); appeals abandoned; co-defendant prosecutions ended; case closed.
- United States v. Donald J. Trump: Immunity framework set by Supreme Court (July 1, 2024); prosecution dismissed without prejudice after the election; case closed.
- State of Georgia v. Donald J. Trump et al.: Prosecutors disqualified; replacement prosecutor declined to proceed; case nolle prossed and dismissed (Nov. 26, 2025).
- Trump v. Anderson: 9–0 reversal of Colorado disqualification; Maine disqualification withdrawn; Trump remained on ballots.
- E. Jean Carroll v. Donald J. Trump: Two plaintiff verdicts totaling $88.3 million; Second Circuit affirmances; Carroll I at Supreme Court petition stage; Carroll II largely final.
- Trump v. United States: Landmark immunity framework announced; underlying D.D.C. case later dismissed without prejudice.
- Fischer v. United States: § 1512(c)(2) limited to evidence-impairment theories; many Jan. 6 obstruction counts required reassessment.
- Optional: Jan. 6 Select Committee referrals & Special Counsel Jack Smith final reports: Referrals informed DOJ charging decisions; federal Trump cases later dismissed; reports are historical DOJ work product.
