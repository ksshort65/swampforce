## 2. Trump — crime graphic, “Whites killed by blacks 81%”

**Verdict: WRONG.**

- **Quote found:** “Whites killed by whites — 16%. Whites killed by blacks — 81%,” (PolitiFact page).
- **Official table:** FBI Expanded Homicide Data Table 6, “Race, Ethnicity, and Sex of Victim by Race, Ethnicity, and Sex of Offender, 2014 [Single victim/single offender].” White-victim row: **total 3,021; white offenders 2,488; Black or African American offenders 446** (other 35; unknown 52).
- **Percentages:** 2,488 / 3,021 = **82.4%** (82% rounded); 446 / 3,021 = **14.8%** (15% rounded). The graphic’s 16% and 81% are wrong.
- **Exact table URL:** https://ucr.fbi.gov/crime-in-the-u.s/2014/crime-in-the-u.s.-2014/tables/expanded-homicide-data/expanded_homicide_data_table_6_murder_race_and_sex_of_vicitm_by_race_and_sex_of_offender_2014.xls
- **Parent FBI page:** https://ucr.fbi.gov/crime-in-the-u.s/2014/crime-in-the-u.s.-2014/offenses-known-to-law-enforcement/expanded-homicide
- **Catalog:** No direct catalog match.
